Photograph, vertical 3:4. Look and era: 1990s fine-art night photography on a medium-format Mamiya 7, Kodak Portra 400 pushed one stop, printed warm. Heavy grain in the shadows, warm sodium-gold and cream tones, deep blacks that still hold a faint trace of texture, highlights softly blooming with halation. The same old grand hotel as the rest of the set: peeling plaster, worn gilt, spotted mirrors, parquet, white tablecloths.

Camera: 43mm wide lens, tripod on the edge of the musicians' gallery high above the ballroom floor, pointing steeply down at the floor, long exposure of about 6 seconds. The carved gallery balustrade crosses one corner of the frame in the foreground, dark and slightly soft.

Scene: 3 am. Seen from high above at a steep angle: the dark herringbone parquet, the edges of a few round tables with white cloths and pushed-back chairs at the borders of the frame, scattered paper streamers. They are not dancing: they have just had a fight. In the single pool of light, a woman in a pale evening dress is walking fast across the floor toward the dark doors at the edge of the frame, one shoe in her hand, her body turned away from him. A step behind her, a man in a dark suit is reaching out for her arm and not quite touching it. Because of the long exposure their movement is smeared into soft translucent streaks in the direction she is walking: her dress trailing, his outstretched arm a blur. The empty dance floor between them and the doors is the tension of the picture.

Light with emphasis: a single source only. About seventy percent of the frame falls into deep darkness; the light lands on the subject and nowhere else, with a steep falloff. No fill light, no evenly lit walls, no bright ceiling. Light: the chandelier hangs between the camera and the floor, out of frame, and throws one round warm pool onto the parquet; the rest of the floor sinks to black at the edges.

Mood: the night has turned. Anger, hurt, the moment before someone leaves. Tense but not violent.

Avoid: evenly lit room, bright walls or ceiling, multiple light sources, spotlights, faces, readable text, logos, horror cues, clean modern interiors, cinematic grading, HDR.
