Photograph, vertical 3:4. Look and era: in the manner of Robert Polidori's large-format photographs of decaying grand interiors (Havana, Versailles) crossed with Hiroshi Sugimoto's long exposures. Shot on a medium-format Mamiya 7 with a 43mm wide lens on Kodak Portra 400, long exposure on a tripod (about 4 seconds). Natural, warm but restrained colour: cream, ivory, faded gold, walnut brown, dusty rose and deep burgundy, with no orange cast and no blue or green. Rich mid-tone detail: every crack of peeling plaster, water stain, worn gilt and fabric texture is readable. Shadows are deep brown-black but still hold detail, never crushed or blotchy. Fine, even film grain; highlights on the light source bloom gently with soft halation.

Camera: tripod at waist height, perfectly level, one-point perspective centred on the room's axis, symmetrical, the polished floor filling the lower part of the frame and softly reflecting the light.

Position: in the centre aisle near the back of an old 1920s picture palace, looking straight down the aisle toward the stage and the closed screen curtain.

Scene: 3 am in a faded grand cinema. Rows of worn burgundy velvet seats on both sides, gilded plaster boxes and peeling mouldings on the side walls, a heavy fringed curtain closed across the screen, popcorn boxes and paper cups scattered on the carpet. Halfway down the aisle, a young usher in a waistcoat is sweeping the carpet with a long broom, bent forward, the broom and their arms swept into a soft arc of motion. Because of the long exposure, the moving person is a soft, translucent motion blur, smeared along the direction of their movement; everything still in the room is perfectly sharp. Dust hangs in the air in the light.

Light: an old crystal chandelier with small candle-shaped bulbs, hanging visibly in the upper centre of the frame, is the main source. It throws a soft warm pool onto the floor below and gently lights the nearby walls so their texture shows, then falls off into darkness toward the corners and ceiling. A few small wall sconces glow faintly along the side walls. No spotlights and no bare bulbs.

Mood: the show ended long ago, and someone is still putting the night away. Quiet, tender, half-remembered.

Avoid: horror cues, faces, readable text, logos, clean modern interiors, orange colour cast, crushed blacks, posterised shadows, spotlights, cinematic grading, HDR.
