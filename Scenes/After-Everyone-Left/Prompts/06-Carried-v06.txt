Photograph, vertical 3:4. Look and era: 1990s fine-art night photography on a medium-format Mamiya 7, Kodak Portra 400 pushed one stop, printed warm. Heavy grain in the shadows, warm sodium-gold and cream tones, deep blacks that still hold a faint trace of texture, highlights softly blooming with halation. The same old grand hotel as the rest of the set: peeling plaster, worn gilt, spotted mirrors, parquet, white tablecloths.

Camera: 43mm lens, tripod at standing eye height just inside the hotel's tall front doors, in the dark entrance hall, looking out through the open doorway; the dark door frame and one open glass door with rain on it fill the left and top of the frame as a black foreground. Long exposure of about 1 second.

Scene: 3 am, rain. Outside, wide stone steps lead down from the hotel to the wet street. Halfway down the steps, the man is carrying the woman in his arms down toward the street: she is barefoot in her pale evening dress, her shoes dangling from her hand, his dark jacket draped over her shoulders, her arm around his neck. They are seen from behind and slightly above, small in the middle of the frame. At the kerb an old black taxi waits with its rear door open, its warm interior light on and its headlights cutting through the rain. The motion and the rain blur them softly; the steps, the door frame and the taxi are sharp. Behind the camera the hotel is dark.

Light with emphasis: a single source only. About seventy percent of the frame falls into deep darkness; the light lands on the subject and nowhere else, with a steep falloff. No fill light, no evenly lit walls, no bright ceiling. Light: one old street lamp beside the steps, warm, rim-lighting the two figures and the falling rain, with the taxi's small warm interior light and headlights as the only other glow; the hotel entrance hall around the camera is black.

Mood: he carries her home. The building stays empty and dark behind them. Tender, bittersweet, the end of the night.

Avoid: evenly lit room, bright walls or ceiling, multiple light sources, spotlights, faces, readable text, logos, horror cues, clean modern interiors, cinematic grading, HDR.
