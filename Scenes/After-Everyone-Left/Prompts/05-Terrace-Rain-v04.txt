Photograph, vertical 3:4. Look and era: 1990s fine-art night photography on a medium-format Mamiya 7, Kodak Portra 400 pushed one stop, printed warm. Heavy grain in the shadows, warm sodium-gold and cream tones, deep blacks that still hold a faint trace of texture, highlights softly blooming with halation. The same old grand hotel as the rest of the set: peeling plaster, worn gilt, spotted mirrors, parquet, white tablecloths.

Camera: 43mm lens, tripod inside the dark ballroom, a few steps back from a tall arched window, framing the window off-centre so the black window frame and a heavy curtain fill the left and lower foreground. Raindrops and streaks on the glass are in soft focus. Long exposure of about 3 seconds.

Scene: 3 am, rain. Inside, nothing is visible but the dark curtain edge and the silhouette of the window frame. Outside, on the stone terrace with a balustrade, under a single old iron lamp, a couple is dancing in the rain, small in the upper right third of the frame, soft and translucent from the long exposure, her dress swinging, the wet stone reflecting the lamp. Beyond the terrace, black night.

Light with emphasis: a single source only. About seventy percent of the frame falls into deep darkness; the light lands on the subject and nowhere else, with a steep falloff. No fill light, no evenly lit walls, no bright ceiling. Light: the terrace lamp outside is the only light, warm, with the rain streaking in its glow; the interior is black.

Mood: the room is empty, and they went outside to keep dancing. Romantic, lonely, a little reckless.

Avoid: evenly lit room, bright walls or ceiling, multiple light sources, spotlights, faces, readable text, logos, horror cues, clean modern interiors, cinematic grading, HDR.
