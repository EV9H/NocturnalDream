NOCTURNAL DREAM / SHANGHAI AFTER THE SNOW
One continuous five-second 9:16 visual scene. Use the supplied v03 still as the first frame.

WORLD & SCALE
An exceptional pre-dawn snowstorm has buried the familiar Bund. Start on the near drift against a waist-high rail post. Old bootprints overlap in the continuous snow, some already softening under fresh powder. Beyond the buried railing, the Huangpu remains wide, dark and open.

VISUAL DISCOVERY & CAMERA
Take two measured steps along the west-bank promenade. The eye-level camera moves gently to the right, parallel to the rail, and stays square to it. The near post slides left faster than the skyline. As a gust thins over the water, snow-loaded Pudong roofs and terraces come into clearer view below the Oriental Pearl. Heavy snowfall continues across both banks.

MIDNIGHT WITNESS
Midnight-blue ambient light, deep readable charcoal and tactile silver-gray snow. A fixed off-frame tungsten lamp behind and to the right grazes the near drift, with a small amber falloff. Hold the dark sky and soft highlight roll-off. Loose powder skims low in uneven gusts. Close flakes sweep past the lens faster than distant flurries. River ripples break up the sparse office-light reflections.

SOUND & CONTINUITY
Two close, muffled snow crunches in time with the steps. Gusts rise and fall over a low river wash and faint city sound across the water. Keep the rail straight, the old tracks grounded and the skyline solid. Preserve the snow depth and steady pre-dawn exposure. No pan, zoom, cuts, music or voices.
