NOCTURNAL DREAM / SHANGHAI AFTER THE SNOW
One continuous 5-second 9:16 shot. Open on the supplied v03 still. Before dawn, a snowstorm has buried both banks of Shanghai's Huangpu. Old boot treads and snow-capped Bund rail posts establish the depth at human scale.

CAMERA & DISCOVERY
Begin level at pedestrian eye height, facing square across the river. Over two measured steps, track about one metre to camera-right, parallel to the railing, at a fixed focal length and heading. Nearby bootprints and the rounded rail post move left across frame. The far bank shifts only slightly. A fresh section of the same buried railing enters from the right. In the final second, a thin veil of snow over the river lets the pale Pudong roof caps and riverwalk reappear beneath the Oriental Pearl as the storm continues.

SNOW, WATER & LIGHT
Wind sweeps from upper right to lower left, with small eddies and uneven bursts. A few near flakes pass as soft, short streaks. Smaller flakes drift beyond the rail, and loose powder skims along its base. Compacted boot treads and the drift hold their shape. The open river breaks office lights into short, wavering vertical traces.

Midnight Witness. Retain v03's dark horizon, readable charcoal and cool silver-gray snow. A fixed off-frame tungsten lamp behind camera-right grazes the drift shoulder. Flakes briefly brighten as they pass through the beam. The warm patch stays on the snow as the camera passes. Keep the near snow texture crisp, the skyline softened by distance, and the exposure steady.

LOCATION SOUND
Two close, soft boot crunches timed to the steps. Wind swells with the passing gust over distant river wash and a faint far-bank city hum. Location sound only. The shoreline, towers and railing retain their shape through the move. No pan, zoom or cut.
