NOCTURNAL DREAM / SHANGHAI AFTER THE SNOW
One continuous eight-second vertical 9:16 shot through a pedestrian's eyes on the west-bank Bund before dawn. Use v03 for the storm, the buried railing, the Pudong skyline and the Midnight Witness look. The opening is a downward glance along the promenade.

OPENING
Walk forward with the river railing to the left. Look down and a little ahead. Dark boot toes enter in alternation. A boot presses into a half-filled tread left by someone earlier and crushes its thin powder rim. The soft crunch lands with a slight dip of the head. A charcoal cuff and gloved hand swing once at the bottom edge. Snow covers the entire promenade, even inside the tracks.

DISCOVERY
At the lower left, one bevel of a granite lantern base and a short section of wave-pattern steel catch the light. The steel disappears into a continuous bank almost level with the top rail. The drift meets the lantern base with a soft contact shadow, and loose grains skim across its surface in the wind.

Lift the chin and turn left toward the Huangpu in one smooth head motion. The feet continue forward along the promenade while the gaze turns. The near rail crosses the frame quickly. Pudong comes in from the left and sweeps right with the turn, its buildings holding their relative spacing. Slow to a stop and hold the river view for the last two to three seconds. Through thinner snow, pale low roof caps and stepped terraces read as a band beneath the Oriental Pearl and the office towers. The dark, open river breaks the office lights into short reflections.

MIDNIGHT WITNESS
Fixed moderate-wide lens and natural eye-height perspective. Deep readable charcoal, midnight-blue ambient fill and cool silver-gray snow. One fixed off-frame tungsten lamp on the promenade grazes the drift from the inland side. Its narrow amber edge briefly catches the granite bevel, a moving cuff and passing flakes. Leave dark sky above the skyline. Keep the near snow tactile and the far shore softer through layered weather. A few close flakes pass softly out of focus. Smaller flurries and low spindrift move in uneven gusts. Hold the geometry and exposure through the turn.

LOCATION SOUND
Close, soft boot crunches and a faint brush of fabric. The footfalls fall away as the walker stops. Wind continues over distant river wash and a low city hum. One take with a continuous shoreline and no cut, dissolve or zoom.
