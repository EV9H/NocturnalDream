NOCTURNAL DREAM / SHANGHAI AFTER THE SNOW
One continuous eight-second 9:16 first-person shot on Shanghai's west-bank Bund in the last dark minutes before dawn. The supplied v03 still is the final view across the Huangpu. The storm has nearly erased the familiar waterfront.

0–3 SECONDS — THE WALK
Walk forward along the promenade, river railing on the left. Look down and a little ahead from adult eye height. The tips of two dark boots enter in turn. One presses into an older heel print, collapsing its fresh powder rim. The entire path is snow-covered. Along the left edge, a waist-high drift has swallowed the foot of a Bund lantern's granite plinth. Only its beveled crown and a short run of the railing's dark wave-pattern steel break the crust. Each step gives the view a small rise and weighted settle.

3–6 SECONDS — THE TURN
Pass the plinth on the same forward line. The walker's left wool glove briefly brushes the drift beside the rail. A small clump crumbles downward and fine grains blow away. The hand drops as the walker lifts their chin and turns left in one continuous head movement. The near post sweeps past faster than the opposite shore. Pudong travels across the frame as the view opens over the dark river.

6–8 SECONDS — ACROSS THE HUANGPU
The walker comes to rest, looking square across the water. A thin veil of snow moves past the far bank. Pale roof caps, snow along the riverwalk and white ledges on the lower terraces emerge beneath the Oriental Pearl and Lujiazui towers. The river remains open, with short, broken office-light reflections. Hold the skyline beneath generous dark sky while the storm continues on both banks.

MIDNIGHT WITNESS
Keep a natural wide-angle perspective. Sharp boot tread and granular snow in the foreground, soft storm haze at a distance. Cool silver-gray snow and midnight-blue ambient light, with depth in the charcoal shadows. A single off-frame tungsten promenade lamp behind and to the right grazes the plinth and glove. Amber catches only the edges and passing flakes, then falls away into shadow. Soft highlight roll-off and restrained grain. Keep rail, shore and skyline in one continuous space with steady exposure.

SOUND
Close powder crunches synced to the steps, a brief wool scrape and soft snow crumble. The footfalls fall away as the view settles on Pudong, leaving uneven wind over low river wash and muted city hum. Location sound only. One take, no cuts, dissolves or zoom.
