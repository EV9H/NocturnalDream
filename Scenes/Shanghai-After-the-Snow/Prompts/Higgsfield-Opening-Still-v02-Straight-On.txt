A vertical 9:16 low-light photograph from the existing pedestrian position on Shanghai’s west-bank Bund promenade, in the last dark minutes before dawn after an exceptional overnight snowfall.

Turn in place to face directly across the Huangpu toward Pudong. The camera is level at normal adult eye height on the promenade, square to the straight section of railing in front of it. The continuous top rail crosses the lower half of the portrait frame horizontally, parallel to the horizon, with upright posts and no diagonal recession. Along the bottom of the frame, a narrow band of wet rectangular paving, compacted snow and old bootprints runs left to right along the promenade.

On the near side of the railing, a wind-rounded waist-high drift rises from the paving to just under the top rail, burying the lower bars in the left bay. Immediately beside it, the right bay is exposed all the way down to its footing. Show the full height of this one bay clearly, with understated wave-pattern steel bars typical of the Bund. Keep the snow bank close to the railing and the paving visible across the foreground.
