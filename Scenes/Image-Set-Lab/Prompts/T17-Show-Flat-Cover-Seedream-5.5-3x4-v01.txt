Photograph, vertical 3:4. Look and era: 2014 fine-art interior photograph on a Linhof 4x5 view camera with a Schneider 90mm lens at f/22, tripod, 8-second exposure under the room's own lights, Kodak Portra 160 sheet film, colour left uncorrected so the tungsten lights stay warm and the outside light stays cold. Fine grain, very slight fall-off at the corners, true straight verticals, no diffusion filter, no added lighting. References: Lynne Cohen's deadpan photographs of empty display and institutional interiors, Thomas Demand's rooms that are almost right, Todd Hido's interiors at night.

Colour palette: champagne beige, peach, walnut brown and brushed brass inside; one cold construction-floodlight blue at the far edge. Nothing saturated.

Camera: frontal and level at about 1.4 metres, standing in the doorway of the show flat's living room, looking straight across the room toward the big window. Symmetrical but not perfect, a little of the door frame soft at the left edge.

Scene: a fully furnished show flat built inside a property developer's sales centre, at 2 am, closed. A beige fabric sofa with a clear plastic protective film still on one arm, a walnut coffee table with a glass bowl of plastic lemons and a stack of decorative books with blank spines, a rug with vacuum lines, a brass floor lamp, a wall-mounted TV that is off, a pair of blue disposable shoe covers dropped by the door. Behind half-open sheer curtains, the "window" is a large printed photograph of a sunny daytime city skyline, lit evenly, so the room shows a bright afternoon at 2 am. Its upper right corner is peeling away, revealing raw plywood and a dusty work lamp behind it. No people.

Light: real fixtures only, all visible. Recessed ceiling downlights make sharp warm cones and circular pools on the rug and coffee table. One track spotlight grazes the printed backdrop, showing its slight sheen and print texture. The floor lamp adds a small warm pool by the sofa. A thin strip of cold blue floodlight from the construction site outside leaks through a gap at the far right. Shadows fall straight down under each downlight; the ceiling corners and the space under the sofa stay dark.

Mood: a home that nobody has ever lived in, waiting with all its lights on. Warm, quiet, slightly false, like a memory of a room.

Avoid: people, readable text, logos, brand names, real windows, night city view, HDR, glossy CGI render look, perfect even lighting, cinematic teal-orange grading, dramatic haze.
