Candid 35mm film photograph, vertical 3:4, taken standing in the middle of a crowded underground metro platform in a big Chinese city during the evening rush, camera at chest height looking straight down the centre of the platform. Central one-point perspective: the yellow tactile safety line, the glass platform screen doors on the right and the long rows of ceiling fluorescent lights all run to a single vanishing point.

It is snowing inside the station. Real snow falls from the ceiling between the light panels and drifts slowly down through the whole platform; thin snow has settled along the yellow line, on top of the platform doors and on the shoulders of people's dark winter coats, and the floor tiles are wet and grey with slush footprints. Commuters keep walking briskly toward the camera and away from it in two flows, blurred by motion, a few holding open umbrellas indoors. One woman in the centre-left has stopped dead and looks up at the ceiling, sharp and still, her breath faintly visible.

Above, a red LED arrival board glows at the top of the frame, its light catching nearby snowflakes. Cool fluorescent white mixed with warm LED, highlights softly glowing with red halation around the bright points, deep blacks in the tunnel.

Shot on CineStill 800T, handheld at 1/15 second, visible grain, soft focus falloff, slight motion smear on walkers, slightly crooked framing. No readable text other than the glowing board, no logos.
