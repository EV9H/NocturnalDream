Photograph, vertical 3:4. Look and era: 2001 photograph on a Contax 645 with an 80mm f/2 lens wide open, handheld from a raised step, 1/30 second with a small on-camera flash dragged at second curtain, Kodak Portra 800 pushed one stop, Tiffen Black Pro-Mist 1/4 filter so the fireworks and lamps halate. Coarse grain, slight motion trails on the lights, natural skin. References: Wong Kar-wai and Christopher Doyle's Fallen Angels for the colour and smear, Andreas Gursky's crowd scale, and Alex Prager's staged mass.

Colour palette, strictly: firework magenta and electric green spilling down from above, deep navy night, warm sodium amber from the streetlamps at the edges, the crowd in dark wool coats of ink, cream and burgundy with a few red scarves. No other colours.

Camera: from a raised step at the edge of a vast riverside city square, about two metres above the crowd, looking slightly down into the middle of it, framing a little tilted. Out-of-focus heads and a raised phone fill the lower foreground edge.

Scene: New Year's Eve, the moment fireworks burst overhead. Tens of thousands of people fill the square to the horizon, every face tilted up toward the sky, washed in magenta and green light, breath visible in the cold. In the exact middle of the frame, one young couple is the only pair not looking up: they stand facing each other, a step apart, her hand on his coat sleeve, their faces turned away from the camera, the moment unreadable as a kiss or a goodbye. The flash catches them a little brighter than the crowd.

Light: the fireworks above give the main colour, magenta and green across the upturned faces; the faint on-camera flash lights the couple and nearby coats; streetlamps add amber at the far edges. The sky's top corner falls to navy black.

Mood: the whole city is looking at the sky, and these two are looking at each other for maybe the last time. Tender, uncertain, overwhelming.

Avoid: posed smiles, grinning faces, cheering open mouths, faces looking at camera, glamour faces, plastic skin, cloned identical people, readable text, logos, countdown clocks with numbers, HDR, CGI look, cinematic teal-orange grading.
