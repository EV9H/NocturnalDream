Candid 35mm film photograph, vertical 3:4, taken at night from inside a narrow 城中村 alley in a southern Chinese city, between tall "handshake" tenement buildings that almost touch, camera at eye height looking up the alley toward the strip of sky.

The full moon hangs impossibly huge and low, filling most of the strip of sky between the buildings, far larger than a real moon, its craters and grey seas clearly visible, slightly overexposed and glowing. Its cold silver light falls into the alley: the tops of the buildings, air-conditioner units, tangled overhead cables and laundry on bamboo poles are rim-lit silver, with long soft shadows down the walls.

In the foreground, lit by direct on-camera flash, a middle-aged auntie in floral cotton pyjamas and plastic slippers stands in the alley holding her phone up to film the moon, her face turned up and lit by the phone screen, mouth slightly open. Behind her, out of focus, two neighbours lean out of a barred window looking up, one hand pointing. A wet concrete floor, a parked electric scooter, a red plastic stool.
