Photograph, vertical 3:4. Look and era: 2006 photograph on a Deardorff 8x10 view camera with a 240mm lens at f/32, tripod, 30-second exposure at night, Kodak Portra 160 sheet film, uncorrected colour so fluorescent light goes green-cyan and sodium light goes deep orange. Very fine grain, soft movement in the fog and in the bus exhaust because of the long exposure, slight halation around each lamp, true straight verticals, no filter. References: Stephen Shore's Uncommon Places for the deadpan frontal roadside view, Todd Hido's fog at night, Joel Sternfeld's colour.

Colour palette: fluorescent green-cyan, sodium orange, the warm yellow of the bus interior, and the grey-blue of fog. Nothing else saturated.

Camera: tripod at standing height on the edge of the car park, about 40 metres back, level, frontal. The cracked asphalt with grass occupies the lower third and is sharp.

Scene: a Chinese highway service area at 3 am in thick low fog. On the left, a petrol station canopy lit from beneath by fluorescent tubes, three pumps, nobody there. In the middle distance, a single long-distance coach is parked with its engine idling, all interior lights on; through its windows many passengers are asleep against the glass and the seat backs, small and soft. Its exhaust drifts and glows in the light. Behind it, the low building of the service area with two lit vending machines by the door and a mop bucket outside the toilet entrance. The car park is otherwise empty; long grass and weeds grow up through the cracks in the asphalt and around the faded parking lines, as if no one has stopped here for years, but every light is on.

Light: real sources only. The canopy throws a hard green-cyan box of light on the forecourt; two tall sodium lamps make orange cones in the fog over the car park; the bus interior glows warm yellow through its windows; the vending machines throw small white pools on the pavement. Between the sources, the fog and the far edges fall to deep blue-black.

Mood: the middle of a long night journey, when everything is lit for nobody. Quiet, lonely, half asleep.

Avoid: readable text, Chinese characters, signs with words, logos, brand names, faces in detail, HDR, glossy CGI look, cinematic teal-orange grading, horror cues, dramatic god rays.
