Photograph, vertical 3:4. Look and era: 2012 photograph on a Pentax 67 with a 45mm f/4 lens at f/11, tripod on a high footbridge, 1/2 second exposure so the moving crowd smears in one direction while anyone standing still stays sharp. Fujifilm Pro 400H, slight underexposure, Tiffen Black Pro-Mist 1/8 filter for a soft glow on the lights, fine grain, cool-green scan with warm highlights. References: Andreas Gursky's mass compositions for the scale, Alex Prager's Face in the Crowd for the colour-controlled crowd, and Jia Zhangke's Still Life for the Chinese everyday realism.

Colour palette, strictly: station fluorescent cyan-white, sodium amber, and a crowd in mustard, teal, navy, coral and grey, almost everyone carrying red bags, red-wrapped gifts and red-and-white striped woven sacks. One young woman in a pale celadon-green coat with no bag is the only celadon in the frame.

Camera: looking straight down at about 60 degrees from a high footbridge inside a huge Chinese railway station waiting hall at night, the crowd filling the whole frame, slightly crooked framing, the footbridge's steel edge soft in the top-left corner.

Scene: the Spring Festival travel rush. Thousands of travellers stream in one direction toward the ticket gates at the top of the frame, all motion-blurred into streaks of colour and red luggage. In the lower-middle third, one young woman walks the opposite way, back toward the exit, sharp and still in the blur, head slightly lowered, hands in her pockets. Rows of plastic seats, scattered newspapers and a dropped orange on the terrazzo floor.

Light: cold cyan-white fluorescent tubes from the high ceiling; a single warm sodium pool falls near the woman so she reads warmer than the crowd. The ceiling and far edges fall into darkness.

Mood: everyone is going home except her. Lonely, quiet, completely understood.

Avoid: posed smiles, faces looking at camera, glamour faces, plastic skin, cloned identical people, readable text, Chinese characters, departure boards with text, logos, HDR, CGI crowd, cinematic teal-orange grading.
