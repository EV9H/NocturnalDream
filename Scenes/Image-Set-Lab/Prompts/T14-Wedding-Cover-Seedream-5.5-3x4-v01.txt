Photograph, vertical 3:4. Look and era: 2009 staged documentary photograph on a Mamiya RZ67 with a 50mm f/4.5 lens at f/8, tripod, 1/15 second, Kodak Portra 400 rated at 320, Tiffen Black Pro-Mist 1/4 filter so the chandeliers and stage lights bloom softly. Fine visible grain, natural skin, slightly warm scan, a little vignetting, one chandelier blown out. References: Alex Prager's Face in the Crowd for the staged mass and colour, Philip-Lorca diCorcia's hidden strobe for the one spotlit subject, and the banquet scenes of Ang Lee's The Wedding Banquet (1993).

Colour palette, strictly: lacquer red, antique gold, deep jade green, cream and ink black. Guests' clothes in muted jade, mustard, cream, burgundy and navy, table by table. No other colours.

Camera: on the upper balcony at the back of a vast old Chinese wedding banquet hall, looking down at about 45 degrees across the whole room toward the stage at the far end, framed slightly off-centre. The balcony's red-lacquered railing runs soft and out of focus across the bottom of the frame.

Scene: a wedding banquet at night, about 60 round tables with red tablecloths and glass lazy Susans full of half-eaten dishes, red wine glasses, small firecracker paper scraps on the carpet. Every guest, hundreds of people, has turned in their chair toward the stage, many holding up phones, as the bride in a red qipao and the groom raise their glasses under a warm spotlight. Only one man at a back table, small in the lower-middle third of the frame, is turned the other way, sitting still with his glass on the table, looking at the bride from the dark. He is the only figure not facing the stage.

Light: one warm golden spotlight on the stage couple, the brightest point in the frame; dim gold chandeliers over the hall; a weak hidden light catches the turned man so he separates from the crowd. The back of the hall falls into red-brown shadow, about 60 percent of the frame dark.

Mood: a celebration where one person is quietly losing someone. Tender, heavy, unspoken.

Avoid: posed smiles, grinning faces, eye contact with camera, glamour retouching, plastic skin, identical cloned guests, readable text or characters, double-happiness signs with readable glyphs, logos, HDR, cinematic teal-orange grading, CGI look.
