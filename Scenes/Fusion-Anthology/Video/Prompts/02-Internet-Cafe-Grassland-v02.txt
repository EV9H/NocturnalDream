One continuous 3:4 shot, 4 seconds, locked-off camera, no camera movement, no cuts, no zoom, no on-screen text, no subtitles.
Image 1 is the first frame and sets the place, the light, the tilted framing and the old phone-camera look with flash, noise and JPEG grain; keep that look for the whole shot.
The camera stays perfectly still at the tilted angle of Image 1. All life comes from the screens and the grass.
0–1s: Right at the start, the big CRT monitor at lower left, beside the instant-noodle cup, degausses: its blue picture shudders, a rainbow ripple of colour sweeps across the glass and bends the image, then the picture snaps back and settles.
1–3s: Out in the grassland, the CRT monitors on the tables in the grass flicker unevenly and out of step: one dims and recovers, another blinks off and on twice. A gust of wind crosses the grass from left to right, bending the blades that are lit by the monitors' blue glow, the nearest blades first.
3–4s: The farthest monitor in the field goes dark and stays dark. Everything else holds still; the grass settles.
Fixed: the camera does not move; the ceiling tiles and the clean edge where the ceiling stops over the field stay in the same place; desks, chairs, keyboards, the green tea bottle and noodle cups keep their shape and position; the fluorescent tube at upper right stays on; the pylon and the small light on the horizon stay fixed against the deep blue night sky; flash exposure and grain stay steady.
Sound: listening from the empty chair beside the nearest desk. At the very first frame, the loud mid-pitched "thunk" and falling buzz of a CRT degaussing, close and dry. Under it, the thin high whine of old CRT monitors, PC fans humming and a faint fluorescent buzz. Outside, a separate wide layer of wind moving through long grass and sparse crickets. When the gust arrives at one second the grass rustle grows and the fan hum thins, as if the room has no walls to hold it. When the far monitor dies, the CRT whine drops away and only wind and crickets remain for the last second. No music, no speech.
