One continuous 3:4 shot, 4 seconds, locked-off camera, no camera movement, no cuts, no zoom, no on-screen text, no subtitles.
Image 1 is the first frame and sets the place, the light, the tilted framing and the old phone-camera look with flash, noise and JPEG grain; keep that look for the whole shot.
The camera stays perfectly still at the tilted angle of Image 1. The mood is quiet and dreamlike; every change is slow and soft.
0–1s: From the very first frame, a steady night breeze is already moving the long grass beyond the ceiling edge in slow, even waves. The blue glow of the monitors standing in the grass breathes very slowly, a little brighter, a little dimmer, each one out of step with the others.
1–3s: The monitors in the grass fade to dark one after another, starting with the farthest on the left and moving right, each taking about half a second to dim, the glass keeping a faint grey afterglow. The grass around each one loses its blue light as it fades.
3–4s: Only the two nearest monitors in the grass still glow, faintly. The grass keeps swaying. The computers inside the room keep glowing steadily.
Fixed: the camera does not move; every change of light happens only inside the glass of the screens, and the plastic monitor casings stay unchanged; the ceiling tiles and the clean edge where the ceiling stops over the field stay in the same place; desks, chairs, keyboards, the green tea bottle and noodle cups keep their shape and position; the fluorescent tube at upper right stays on; the pylon and the small light on the horizon stay fixed against the deep blue night sky; flash exposure and grain stay steady.
Sound: very quiet and muffled, as if heard half-asleep through a wall. A soft, low, even hum of many old computers blends with a gentle wind through long grass, hollow and far away, with faint crickets in the distance. As the monitors in the grass fade, the hum thins slowly until only the soft wind and crickets remain. Everything stays soft and even from the first frame to the last, with no sudden sounds, no clicks, no beeps and no thuds. No music, no speech.
