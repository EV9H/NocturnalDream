## The first-second rule for this set (Douyin / TikTok)

A viewer decides in about one second, thumb already moving. For these clips that means:

1. **Frame 0 shows the impossibility whole.** Room, seam and landscape are all in the first frame, so we start on the composed still and never on a detail (no tilt-down openings, no approach). The still *is* the hook image.
2. **Something moves softly before 0.5 s.** A first second that looks like a photo reads as a 图文 post and gets swiped. Each clip has one motion already happening at frame 1, and its hero event lands in the first second or very close to it, not at 2–3 s.
3. **Sound is a quiet, muffled bed from frame 1 — no hits.** *(Revised after 02 v02 failed, 2026-11-23: the owner rejected the loud degauss thunk as un-dreamcore.)* The sound is present from the first frame, so it never fades in from silence. It stays soft and continuous, as if heard half-asleep or through a wall. It has no thunks, clicks or ticks, and it leaves room for the Douyin music. The hook is visual and gentle.
4. **Memory trigger over spectacle.** The hook is the sound/motion this audience lived with (网吧 CRT, 超市 freezers at night, 老式卫生间), meeting a landscape that should not be there.
5. **Safe zones.** Hero motion sits away from the bottom ~20% and right edge (UI). Checked against each still below.
6. **End near the start.** Each clip ends close to its first frame (fixed or returning camera), so the cut and any loop stay smooth.

Implication for the cut: the clip with the strongest 0.0 s event should open the video. On this plan that is **02 (degauss)**, not 01; 01's walk-out works better second. Proposal only — decide after seeing the takes.

## 02 · Internet café → night grassland (fixed)

> **Superseded by v03 (2026-11-23).** v02 failed: the degauss thunk was too loud for dreamcore, and the colour ripple spilled onto the monitor casing. See `Prompts/02-Internet-Cafe-Grassland-v03.txt` and the take log in `PRODUCTION.md`.

**Still checked:** near-left CRTs with noodle cups, a green tea bottle, headphones; empty leather chairs; ceiling stops at a hard edge; ~10 CRTs on tables out in the grass under a blue night sky; a pylon and a light on the horizon; a fluorescent tube at upper right.

**Camera:** fixed, tripod-still, at the still's tilted angle. **Why:** the frame already holds two layers of screens (in the room and out in the field); a fixed camera lets their light do the work, and the owner asked for screen life rather than camera travel. Kept from the starting proposal, but the hero changes from a scan-roll to a **degauss**, because a degauss is a single, visible, audible event that anyone who sat in a 网吧 recognises instantly, and it can land at 0.3 s.

**Motion (beats):**
- 0.0–0.8 s: the large CRT at lower-left (with the noodle cup) degausses: the picture shudders and a rainbow ripple sweeps across it, then it settles.
- 0.8–3.0 s: out in the grass the monitors flicker unevenly, one dims and recovers, one blinks twice; a wind gust crosses the grass from left to right and the blades bend in the monitors' blue light.
- 3.0–4.0 s: the farthest monitor in the field goes dark and stays dark. Everything else holds.
- Fixed: ceiling edge, desks, chairs, the tea bottle, horizon and pylon; exposure steady except the screens; the fluorescent tube stays on.

**Sound:**
- Listening point: the empty chair beside the near desk.
- Room: close CRT high whine and PC fan hum, a faint buzz from the fluorescent tube.
- Outside: wind moving through long grass, sparse crickets, low and wide.
- Seam: the gust at 1 s brings the grass forward and the fan hum thins, as if the room's walls aren't holding the sound in.
- Hero: the degauss "thunk" plus a falling hum at 0.0–0.8 s. Silence: after the far monitor dies at ~3 s, the whine drops out and only wind and crickets remain for the last second.
- "No music, no speech."

## 06 · Supermarket → snow plain (handheld, no hands)

**Still checked:** checkout counter and a black basket in the left foreground; the aisle runs back under fluorescent tubes; a long row of chest freezers runs out of the building along the snow; a snow drift with **footprints** covers the floor in the foreground; a pylon at left, two lit apartment blocks at right on the horizon.

**Camera:** handheld from behind the checkout, standing height, no hands in frame. **Why it replaces the trolley POV:** the trolley and hands are not in the first frame, so the model would have to invent them on frame 1 (morph risk, F1), and pushing a trolley forward is the same forward travel the owner rejected. The footprints are already in the still: following them tells a story (someone walked out of the shop into the snow) with a short re-aim, no travel.

**Motion (beats):**
- 0.0–1.0 s: start on the composed frame with handheld breathing. At frame 1 a low ribbon of spindrift is already sliding in from the right across the snow on the floor; one fluorescent tube over the aisle flickers once at ~0.4 s.
- 1.0–2.5 s: the aim dips down and right onto the footprints in the floor snow, then follows them along the freezer row as they lead out past the edge of the roof.
- 2.5–4.0 s: the aim lifts to the snow plain and the two lit apartment blocks, and settles. Breath only.
- Fixed: roof edge, freezer row, the basket, pylon and apartment blocks; the footprints keep their positions; tubes world-fixed.

**Sound:**
- Listening point: behind the till, as the shop worker would hear it.
- Room: freezer compressors humming close, a fluorescent buzz, a small plastic creak from the basket.
- Outside: thin cold wind over open snow, very little else.
- Seam: as the aim follows the footprints out, the compressors fall behind and the wind takes over; one compressor clicks off at ~3 s.
- Hero (revised, no hits): the soft dry hiss of spindrift sliding over the floor, from frame 1. The compressor hum fades out gently around 3 s rather than clicking off; just wind for the final second. The tube flicker stays silent.
- "No music, no speech."

## 07 · Bathroom → tide (fixed, from inside the bathroom)

**Still checked:** wall gas water heater, toothbrush mugs, a hanging towel at left; red plastic stool and pink washbasin on a stand in the foreground; a **floral cloth door curtain** at the right edge, close to the lens; the doorway opens onto wet sand with the clawfoot tub, shower rail and curtain outside; small waves; a lighthouse on the horizon. **There is no tap in frame.**

**Camera:** fixed at the still's angle. **Why:** a doorway frame is a natural proscenium; the sea should come to the camera rather than the camera going to the sea.

**Changes from the starting proposal and why:**
- The tap drip is dropped: no tap is visible, and inventing one adds an object the model must place.
- The near floral door curtain becomes the frame-1 motion (it is large and close, so it reads at phone size); the shower curtain outside lifts too, more softly.
- New hero: **a wave crosses the threshold.** The sea entering the room is one big physical event, and it is the clip's alteration made visible.

**Motion (beats):**
- 0.0–1.0 s: at frame 1 the floral curtain at right is already lifting inward in a sea breeze; the shower curtain by the tub sways after it.
- 1.0–3.0 s: a wave runs up the sand between the tub's feet, reaches the door sill, and a thin sheet of water slides over it onto the bathroom tiles, foam at its edge, stopping short of the red stool.
- 3.0–4.0 s: the water draws back over the sill, leaving the tiles darker and glistening. The curtain falls back. The lighthouse blinks once.
- Fixed: door frame, tub, rail, heater, stool, basin; lighthouse in the same place; flash on the tub stays steady.

**Sound:**
- Listening point: just inside the bathroom, beside the stool.
- Room: small tiled-room reverb, the faint tick of the gas heater, cloth rustle of the near curtain.
- Outside: small waves breaking and pulling on sand, distant and wide.
- Seam: the outside wave has no reverb until the water crosses the sill; then its hiss on the tiles picks up the room's echo. The sound "comes inside".
- Hero (revised, kept soft): a gentle foam hiss as the water spreads over the tiles at ~2 s, low in level and with no splash. Silence: a beat as the water stops, then a quiet draw-back trickle.
- "No music, no speech."
