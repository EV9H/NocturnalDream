A porter carries firewood along the Wall toward the beacon engine. The engine lights its signal, and the engines on the far ridges answer one after another.

0-4s: The porter climbs the wet walkway with the bending shoulder pole, the two bundles of firewood swinging slightly with each step. The camera follows at his pace. Above the watchtower, the engine's crown stands dark against the grey cloud.
4-12s: He walks on and the camera lets him draw a few metres ahead. The walkway levels and turns, and the whole beacon engine comes into view to the right of the watchtower, standing in its walled yard, the water wheel at its foot turning and spilling white water.
12-17s: He stops and lowers the two bundles onto the wet bricks, then straightens and looks up at the engine. Nothing moves except his breath, the cloud and the turning wheel.
17-22s: The ring of bronze vanes at the top begins to turn, slowly and heavily. Fire climbs into two of the five iron bowls, and the other three stay dark. Orange light spreads unevenly across the underside of the cloud above it, following the folds of the cloud. A column of grey smoke rises.
22-27s: On the nearest far ridge to the right, a smaller engine lights two points of fire, then the next ridge, then the farthest, each after an uneven pause, each with a faint warm patch on the cloud above it.
27-30s: The porter lifts the pole back onto his shoulder and walks on toward the yard. Hold.
