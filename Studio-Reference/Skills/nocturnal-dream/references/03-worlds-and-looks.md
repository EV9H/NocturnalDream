# 03 · Worlds and looks

A **look** is a complete photographic world: light, color, lens, texture, motion and sound that serve one emotional question. Choose it from the idea, never from habit. Do not use the same look twice in a row across scenes.

Each card gives the question it answers, the recipe, a prompt block to adapt, and how it fails. Reference artists are for the director to study, not to name in prompts as a substitute for decisions. Verify any reference film before relying on it.

## How to choose

1. State what the viewer must see clearly and what they should discover late.
2. Pick the look whose emotional question matches.
3. Borrow at most one specific device from a second look, and say why.
4. A user-named look or time of day (daylight, bright color, empty space) overrides the studio's habits. A request for "bright" or "daytime" governs the whole scene.

## Look cards

### 1. Overcast Realism 冷峻日光
**Question:** What does an extraordinary condition feel like when the day is ordinary?
- **Light:** flat overcast sky as a giant softbox, low contrast, readable detail everywhere, cool-neutral.
- **Palette:** desaturated greys, concrete, one muted color from a real object (a red plastic stool, a blue shutter).
- **Lens/camera:** 28–35mm equivalent, eye height, locked off or very slow tracking, deep focus.
- **Texture:** fine grain, matte surfaces, visible wear.
- **Sound:** wide room tone, distant traffic, no music or a single sustained tone.
- **Study:** Jia Zhangke (Still Life), Andreas Gursky, Edward Burtynsky, Hiroshi Sugimoto.
- **Block:** `Flat overcast daylight, low contrast, desaturated concrete and steel, one muted red accent, 28mm lens at eye height, locked off, fine film grain, matte surfaces with visible wear.`
- **Fails when:** nothing in the frame has scale or consequence, so it becomes a flat photograph. Add a human-size cue and one strong shape.

### 2. Night Noir 午夜见证
**Question:** What is happening in the dark that someone is quietly watching?
- **Light:** one or two practical sources (sodium street lamp, shop sign, window, headlights), deep but readable shadows, wet surfaces catch narrow reflections.
- **Palette:** charcoal, midnight blue, warm amber or sodium orange as the single accent; keep skin and faces readable.
- **Lens/camera:** 35–50mm, low-key framing, slow lateral track or locked off.
- **Texture:** shadow noise, halation on real lamps only, rain or haze to show light shafts.
- **Sound:** close small sounds (steps, fabric, drips), distant city, low beds.
- **Study:** Darius Khondji, Greig Fraser (The Batman), Wong Kar-wai night scenes.
- **Block:** `Night, one sodium streetlamp camera-right behind the subject, wet asphalt reflecting a narrow orange streak, deep charcoal shadows with readable texture, cool blue sky ambient, 40mm lens, locked off, shadow noise, halation on the lamp only.`
- **Fails when:** all midnight blue, no shapes, or every scene looks like this one. Use only when the scene is about watching or secrecy.

### 3. Humid Nostalgia 潮湿怀旧
**Question:** What did this place feel like to someone who is no longer there?
- **Light:** window light through glass, curtains, steam or rain; warm tungsten from inside against cool green-grey outside; soft halation on highlights.
- **Palette:** desaturated teal-green, aged amber, burgundy fabrics, faded paint.
- **Lens/camera:** 50–85mm, shallow focus with layered foreground, slow lateral drift with parallax, framing through doorways and glass.
- **Texture:** condensation, peeling paint, warm film grain, slight diffusion.
- **Sound:** rain, old radio, distant TV, floor creaks. Music only if it is the place's own.
- **Study:** Christopher Doyle and Mark Lee Ping Bin (In the Mood for Love), Fan Ho, Saul Leiter.
- **Block:** `Warm tungsten room seen through a rain-streaked window, teal-green outside, aged amber inside, burgundy curtain edge in foreground, 65mm lens, shallow focus, slow lateral drift creating parallax, condensation on glass, warm film grain.`
- **Fails when:** haze is applied as a filter, faces melt, and there is no specific object that carries the memory.

### 4. Megastructure Sublime 巨构
**Question:** How small is a person next to the system that holds the city together?
- **Light:** a single dominant source cut by architecture (shaft of sun, furnace glow, lantern grid), strong atmospheric depth, backlit haze.
- **Palette:** big areas of adjacent, desaturated color (rust, ash, bronze), one hot accent.
- **Lens/camera:** wide to normal (24–35mm), human-scale foreground cue, slow move that reveals planes, or locked off waiting for motion to cross a fixed reference.
- **Texture:** rivets, patina, steam, dust, wet metal, cloth, people-sized details.
- **Sound:** low-frequency drone, machine rhythm, metal ticks, wind through lattices.
- **Study:** Deakins and Villeneuve (Blade Runner 2049), Greig Fraser (Dune), Burtynsky, Gursky.
- **Block:** `A bronze ring the size of a mountain turning slowly above a tiled-roof district, backlit steam and dust haze, one shaft of low sun cutting across the foreground roof, 28mm lens, people on the roof ridge for scale, rust and bronze palette, locked off.`
- **Fails when:** scale cues are missing and everything is brown fog. Put a known-size object in the same plane as the subject.

For ancient-China steampunk or hybrid worlds, read [11](11-ancient-china-megastructure.md) first.

### 5. Liminal Dream 梦核 / 阈限
**Question:** What if a familiar place were still there, but time behaved as it does in memory?
- **Light:** fluorescent or daylight at the wrong hour, flat and slightly sickly, with unusual shadow direction, no hero lighting.
- **Palette:** institutional pastel (hospital green, school beige), faded, with one slightly wrong color.
- **Lens/camera:** 24–28mm, long corridors, symmetric or deep one-point perspective, slow forward drift or locked off. Emptiness is the subject.
- **Texture:** low-contrast, slight noise, plastic and linoleum with believable wear.
- **Sound:** room tone, buzzing light, distant school-broadcast music, footsteps echoing.
- **Study:** Bi Gan (long-take dream sequences), Tarkovsky, 童年校园记忆.
- **Block:** `Empty school corridor at 4pm, flat fluorescent and pale daylight, institutional green walls, linoleum with scuffs, 24mm lens, one-point perspective, a distant 课间操 broadcast, a single door at the end slightly ajar, low contrast, slight noise.`
- **Fails when:** it is just an empty corridor. Add a rule (what time is doing) and one trace of the people who should be there.

### 6. Editorial Tableau 编辑式装置
**Question:** What if one thing in an orderly arrangement behaved impossibly?
- **Light:** sculptural, directional, hard sources, strong shadow shapes, restrained palette with one accent.
- **Palette:** charcoal, silver, one saturated accent (burgundy, amber, jade).
- **Lens/camera:** 35–50mm, composed like a still photograph, locked off, symmetry allowed when it expresses order.
- **Texture:** clean but real: seams, dust, scuffs, fabric weave.
- **Sound:** mostly silent room with one precise, physical sound for the impossible event.
- **Study:** Gregory Crewdson, Philip-Lorca diCorcia, Yorgos Lanthimos's staging, Wes Anderson only for compositional discipline.
- **Block:** `A tea-house hall lit by a single hard window from camera-left, long diagonal shadow on polished wood, charcoal and silver with one jade-green teapot, 40mm lens, locked off, a stream of tea rising upward from the cups in a straight line, dust in the window beam.`
- **Fails when:** it looks like a commercial set. Give the space a real use and a flaw.

### 7. Golden Naturalism 诗意自然光
**Question:** What does tenderness or release feel like in real light?
- **Light:** low sun, backlight, window light, wind-moved leaves; shadows keep texture; flares only where the lens would show them.
- **Palette:** natural warm-cool, skin tones intact, earthy greens, dust.
- **Lens/camera:** 35mm, handheld with breath or a following camera, long takes, natural depth of field.
- **Texture:** fine grain, soft highlight roll-off, motion blur real to shutter.
- **Sound:** wind, birds, fabric, footsteps, breath, one distant human sound.
- **Study:** Emmanuel Lubezki, Terrence Malick, Robbie Ryan, Hiro Murai.
- **Block:** `Late afternoon sun low behind the subject, backlit dust and hair, 35mm handheld with gentle breathing motion, warm gold against cool green shadow, natural shallow focus, wind moving fabric, fine film grain.`
- **Fails when:** every frame is a sunset. Vary time of day, and make the light's motion physical.

### 8. Operatic Color 浓色
**Question:** What if the feeling is so large that the world turns the color of it?
- **Light:** controlled color from real sources: neon sign, stage light, red lantern, glowing screen; color blocks with clear shapes.
- **Palette:** one dominant saturated color (red, jade, indigo) in several values, plus a neutral.
- **Lens/camera:** 35–50mm, graphic framing, symmetric or tableau composition, slow deliberate moves.
- **Texture:** silk, lacquer, wet street, smoke; keep highlights controlled.
- **Sound:** spare music from the space, close breath or fabric, stage noise.
- **Study:** Zhang Yimou (Hero, color-coded worlds), Wong Kar-wai, Gaspar Noé only for color staging, The Neon Demon for color-as-set.
- **Block:** `A narrow alley washed in a single red from hanging lanterns, deep red shadows and bright orange highlights on wet cobblestone, a silhouetted figure at the far end, 35mm lens, symmetrical framing, slow forward drift, smoke at ground level.`
- **Fails when:** colored lights compete everywhere. One dominant color, one clear shape.

## Mixing rule

Pick one look. A second look may lend one device (for example "Humid Nostalgia window glass in an Overcast Realism street"). Write it as one line in the treatment. Do not blend two entire looks, which produces the average of both.

## World directions (story spaces)

Look chooses the camera. A world direction chooses the story space. Combine each with one look.

- **Cold realism.** Modern life under one exceptional condition. Quiet, alienated, pressured. Needs a human-scale cue and one adaptation visible in the frame.
- **Dystopian realism.** A rule of society or infrastructure alters daily life, calmly. Evidence over spectacle: queues, signs, repairs, forms.
- **Dreamcore / liminal.** A familiar public space replays with memory logic. Emptiness plus one trace of presence.
- **Epic megastructure.** Engineering or architecture is the protagonist. Can be modern industry or ancient China's alternate technology history (cyberpunk, steampunk or hybrid). Draw from gates, canals, seasonal calendars and market streets, and show how ordinary people live under it.
- **Romantic memory.** Two moments in one place meet. Attachment or missed chance. Light and objects carry the link.
- **Surreal installation.** One exhibit, stage or display logic changes form. The place reveals another use.
- **Humanist wonder.** An imagined change connects specific people to the everyday. Use when asked for warmth.
