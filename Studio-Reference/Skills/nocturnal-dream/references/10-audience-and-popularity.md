# 10 · Audience, popularity and STEPPS

Why would a Douyin viewer stop, finish, rewatch, comment or send this to someone? Every claim here is a **hypothesis** from general knowledge of short-video platforms and the STEPPS framework (Berger). None has been measured on this account. Test against real data and replace guesses with results.

## The waste-of-time test

After one viewing, the viewer should have gained at least one of these. If none, the film is a waste of their time, however pretty.

1. **A feeling they can name** ("好怀念", "好安静", "好震撼").
2. **A memory unlocked** ("这就是我们学校的广播").
3. **Something to say to a specific person** ("发给 XX 看").
4. **A "wait, what?" worth a second viewing** that explains itself on rewatch.

Pretty images without one of these are wallpaper.

## Why AI scenes are or are not interesting

- Viewers know it is AI and the novelty of "AI made this" is gone. Photoreal AI alone no longer earns attention. Generic AI fantasy (neon cities, floating islands, glossy snow) reads as slop and is skipped.
- What AI can do that real footage cannot: show a **memory or an impossible condition at real-photograph fidelity**. A school field full of afterimages, a wall that remembers hands, a flooded Bund as routine. The idea is the product. AI is the camera.
- What wins: **recognition first, impossibility second, emotion last.** The viewer must first think "I know this place", then "but it's different", then feel something about the difference. In the best case the first two happen in the same second.
- **Reach comes from recognition across generations, comments come from cohort memory.** A place every generation knows, shown in a condition it can never have (the Bund under metre-deep snow), needs no setup and reaches the whole feed. An experience one generation or region shares (校园广播, 回南天, 筒子楼) produces more personal comments, but only that cohort recognises it. Choose on purpose and state the cohort. This replaces the earlier "shared memory beats spectacle" rule, following the owner's direction of 2026-11-19 based on Shanghai After the Snow and The School That Never Lets Out. Impossible-but-real spectacle is not slop when the place is specific and the physics are exact. Generic fantasy still is.
- **AI labelling is mandatory.** Since 2025-09-01, China's 《人工智能生成合成内容标识办法》 requires an explicit label on AI-generated content, and removing or hiding a platform's label is prohibited. Always post with Douyin's AI label on. Most of Douyin's 100M-play AI hits in H1 2026 were labelled, so the label is not a barrier to reach. That last point is an inference. See [the market analysis](../../../Market-Analysis-2026-11.md).
- **Real brands, real people, real IP.** Douyin's April 2026 crackdown targets AI likeness abuse, copyright infringement, impersonation for traffic and fake personas selling products. Do not show real trademarks or logos in fabricated ads, real people's faces or voices, or recreations of specific copyrighted works. Use recognisable but unbranded designs.

## What the platform tends to reward (hypotheses, verify)

Short-video recommendation systems usually test a clip on a small audience, then widen it based on:

- **Early drop-off** (the first 1–3 seconds). A weak opening kills reach.
- **Completion rate** and **replays**. Short, dense, loopable clips help.
- **Interaction depth:** likes < favorites < comments < shares < follows, roughly in rising value.
- **Comment activity.** Questions viewers answer from their own life produce comment volume.

Design to earn each:

| Signal | Design lever |
| --- | --- |
| Stay past 3 seconds | Hook frame ([09](09-douyin-and-resolution-tiers.md)) |
| Finish | A payoff within 8–15 seconds, no dead lead-in |
| Replay | A detail visible only on second viewing, or a loop |
| Comment | A shared-memory question the viewer answers without being asked (their school, their city, their grandmother's street) |
| Share | A "send to someone who would get this" line: a place, a generation, a joke |
| Favorite | Beauty or a reference image they want to keep |
| Follow | A recognizable series signature: look, sound motif, title format |

## STEPPS as a design tool

Choose two or three for each scene. Write them as testable hypotheses.

| Lever | Question | Douyin mechanism | Typical failure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Social Currency** | Does sharing it make the sender look perceptive or tasteful? | An unexpected, original image or insight that makes a friend say "你怎么找到的" | Showy tech, "look what AI can do" |
| **Triggers** | What ordinary moment will remind people of it? | Link to a recurring cue: rain season, school bell, Sunday evening, the new-term month | A one-off with no memory hook |
| **Emotion** | What physical feeling follows? | High-arousal emotions spread: awe, nostalgia, tenderness, shock. Low-arousal (calm, wistful) needs identification to travel | Pretty but empty |
| **Public** | Can it be seen, copied or recognised in public? | A signature visual or sound, a repeatable format, a challenge hook | Too complex to describe or imitate |
| **Practical Value** | Is it useful to keep or forward? | Occasional behind-the-scenes or tool tips as a minor part of the feed | Tutorials replacing the work |
| **Stories** | Can people retell it? | A single sentence of cause and effect ("这面墙会记住摸过它的人") | An elaborate setting with no retellable line |

For this studio the strongest levers are probably **Emotion (nostalgia, awe), Triggers (shared sensory memory) and Social Currency (a specific recognition)**. Treat that as a hypothesis.

## Pitch check (add to every pitch)

- **Who exactly is the viewer?** Name a group (上海弄堂长大的, 90后小学生, 在外地的江浙人).
- **Generation reach.** Which birth cohorts recognise the anchor in one second? Write "all ages" or name the range (for example "born 1990–2000 only"). A narrow range is allowed but must be chosen on purpose.
- **What do they comment?** Write the first three likely comments.
- **Who do they send it to, and with what words?**
- **Does it work for someone outside that group?** Not required, but note it.
- **What is the one-sentence retell?**

## Slate strategy

- Choose a recurring **format name** and signature (a title style, a sound motif, a look rule) so a viewer can recognise the next one.
- Alternate shared-memory scenes (comment engines) with pure-art scenes (favorites and follows), and watch which performs.
- Release a few scenes in the same week before drawing conclusions.
- **Consistency is rewarded.** At its September 2026 creator conference, Douyin said it backs creators who keep producing quality work, not accounts with a single hit. Plan for a steady cadence rather than rare hero posts.
- **Get several posts from each paid render.** From one approved generation, cut a hero version, a loop or alternate-angle version from spare takes, and an inexpensive companion post (a world-rule card, or a breakdown of how the scene was made). Proposal, untested. See [the market analysis](../../../Market-Analysis-2026-11.md).
- **Prefer series that can repeat.** Pick one place type and one family of impossible conditions, for example a city plus weather it can never have, so each episode reaches a new local audience and the format stays recognisable.
