# Nocturnal Dream: Douyin market analysis

Date: 2026-11-19. Brief: [Market-Analysis-Prompt.md](Market-Analysis-Prompt.md).

**How this was done, and its limits.** This is a quick desk pass: about 15 web searches in Chinese and English, plus 4 articles read in full. I did not have 蝉妈妈, 飞瓜, 巨量算数 or 星图 back-end access. I could not open Douyin video pages directly, so every view and follower number comes from media reports and was not checked live. The sources over-represent hits, which is survivorship bias. Treat this report as a map for the first 90 days and confirm the key numbers in the Douyin app yourself (section 6).

Labels: **[M]** measured or official data · **[C]** vendor, media or creator claim · **[I]** my inference or estimate.

## 1. Executive summary

AI is Douyin's fastest-growing content type in 2026 **[M, Douyin VP, 2026-09-19]**, and the platform says it now rewards creators who keep producing quality work, not one-off hits. The big AI hits of H1 2026 were human-emotion and nostalgia stories, guofeng, and sci-fi. Most ran from minutes to 19 minutes and featured people **[C, 新榜]**. That backs hypothesis 2. It also means the current format (10–15 s, no people, muted) sits far from the proven hit pattern. Expect smaller reach per post **[I]**.

Shared-memory nostalgia is mainstream: the 中式梦核 topic on 小红书 has more than 500M views **[C, 中新网, 2026-07]**. That is a real audience, but its home base looks like 小红书 more than Douyin **[I]**.

**Recommendation: Option D, weighted toward A.** Keep the cinematic AI scenes as the brand (A). Change three things:
1. Build them as **named series**, not one-off scenes.
2. Get **3 posts a week out of each paid render**: hero cut, loop or alternate cut, and a cheap B-format "how this memory was built" or lore card.
3. Test one **30–60 s micro-story** a month against the 15 s format.

Cross-post to 小红书 from day one. Put off Blender (C) until something has traction.

## 5. 90-day plan (2026-11-24 → 2027-02-22)

**Cadence:** 3 posts a week, about 36 in total, from about 2–3 paid hero renders a month. Each render yields:
1. Hero cut, 10–15 s
2. Loop or alternate-angle cut, 6–8 s, made from leftover takes
3. A B-format companion of 15–30 s: a lore card, "the rule of this world", or a breakdown of how the memory was built

That breakdown also plays to Douyin's push for content with a human touch **[I]**.

**Hooks:**
- Frame one is already wrong. The alteration shows at 0 s, not 5 s.
- The caption asks a memory question: "你们学校的广播也这样吗？" ("Did your school's broadcast sound like this too?")
- The pinned comment invites people to name their own city or school.

**Always on:**
- Show the AI label. It has been mandatory since 2025-09-01 **[M]**.
- Use original sound.
- Cross-post to 小红书 the same day.

**Measure each post at 48 h:**
- 2 s and 5 s drop-off, average watch percentage and completion, from 创作者中心
- Shares per 1k views
- Comments per 1k views
- Follows per 1k views

**Experiments:**

| # | Test | Continue if | Kill if |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | 15 s no-people vs 30–60 s micro-story (one person or a trace of one) | Story wins on shares/1k by more than 1.5× | No difference after 3 pairs: stay short |
| E2 | Muted grade vs warmer, more saturated grade, same scene | Either one wins completion by more than 10% | — |
| E3 | B companion posts | Follows/1k is at least half the hero posts' rate | Under a quarter after 6 posts: drop B |
| E4 | Series naming ("城市记忆档案 #03") vs standalone posts | Series posts beat standalone on follows/1k | — |
| E5 | 小红书 vs Douyin, same asset | Platform with more saves/1k gets priority | — |
| **90-day gate** | Overall account | ≥1 post over 100k views, or ≥3k followers: keep going and scale | <1k followers and no post over 20k: rethink format, likely toward E1's story format |

**First 10 post ideas** (scene, then the hypothesis it tests):
1. School broadcast plays to an empty campus at dusk; the speakers keep going after the bell (H3: quiet, no people).
2. 回南天 (humid season): the corridor wall weeps into a slow indoor rain (H1: everyday shared memory).
3. 国风 water-clock machine the size of 陆家嘴, ticking at dawn (H2: guofeng plus awe).
4. Classroom where each desk shows a different year's graffiti layer (H1).
5. Same corridor, 15 s vs 45 s with a child's footsteps and backpack (E1, H3).
6. 新村 compound where every window shows the same 1998 evening TV program (H1 and comments: "我家也是").
7. 弄堂 laundry lines stretching into the clouds (H1 and H4 hook test).
8. Lore card: "In this world, the school bell rings once for every person who has forgotten it" (E3, B format).
9. Ancient 水运仪象台 rebuilt over the Huangpu, at night (H2, guofeng).
10. Exam morning: the school gate turns as large as a city gate, parents waiting outside (H2: human emotion, minimal people).

## 7. Platform mechanics (verified vs folklore)

**AI labelling**
- **[M]** Mandatory explicit and implicit AI labels since 2025-09-01 (CAC 《人工智能生成合成内容标识办法》). Removing or hiding a platform's AI label is prohibited.

**Enforcement**
- **[M/C]** Douyin's April 2026 crackdown targets AI likeness abuse, copyright infringement, impersonation and selling through fake AI personas. It reported 538k videos removed and 4,000+ accounts penalised.
- **[M/C]** The January 2026 crackdown covered 魔改 film and TV edits.
- Original scenes without real people or IP sit outside these risk areas **[I]**.

**Recommendation system**
- **[M]** In September 2026 Douyin said quality is "the key" to recommendation and that it supports creators who keep producing.
- **[C]** Third-party writers say the system predicts 100+ behaviours (completion, likes, comments, saves, follows, shares). Douyin has not confirmed this. Figures like "200–500 initial pool" are folklore.

**Length, hook timing, posting time**
- No official data found.
- 2026 hit evidence leans toward longer stories, not 15 s clips **[C]**.

## 8. Monetisation and growth

**Platform money**
- 星图 is Douyin's official brand-deal marketplace and its main source of brand deals **[M]**.
- Current entry thresholds were not verified.
- The Douyin × 即梦 AIGC programme (2026-03) co-invests in works: 50–70%, up to ¥2–5M per title **[C]**. This targets short drama, not this format.
- The Douyin AI Creation Contest (2026-06 to 09) drew about 170k entries. More than 6,000 passed 1M plays and more than 300 passed 10M **[C]**. Contests like this are the cheapest visibility route for a small account **[I]**.

**Earnings**
- Top AI accounts reportedly made ¥4M+ within 3 months **[C, unverified, survivorship bias]**.
- A realistic small-account path: contest prizes, then 小红书/Douyin brand deals from about 10k–50k followers (cultural-tourism and local-life brands fit "city memory"), then commissions for city and tourism boards **[I]**.

**Timeline**
- Comparable winners went from zero to 500k–1M in 3–6 months. Those are the survivors.
- Estimate for this account: 10k followers in 3–9 months *if* one post breaks 500k. Most accounts never get there **[I]**.

**Cross-posting**
- 小红书: high value, because 中式梦核 is strongest there.
- B站: good for longer cuts and breakdowns.
- 视频号: older audience, so it suits nostalgia.
- TikTok international: weak cultural-anchor fit.

## 9. Risks

- **AI fatigue / "AI 味".** Template-looking AI video is drawing complaints (克劳锐 via 36氪, 2026-08) **[C]**. Globally, enthusiasm for AI creator content fell from 60% to 26% between 2023 and 2025 (eMarketer) **[C]**. Mitigations: show authorship, keep a distinctive sound, avoid trending templates.
- **Vendor dependence.** Seedance, 即梦 and 小云雀 are all ByteDance. Prices and access can change. Keep prompts portable and test Kling as a fallback.
- **Saturation.** AI short drama is flooded and platforms are removing weak titles (红果 removed tens of thousands) **[C]**.
- **Cadence and burnout.** At 1–3 renders a month, the account depends on derivative cuts. The September 2026 platform message penalises sporadic posting **[I from M]**.
