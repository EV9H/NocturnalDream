# NOCTURNAL DREAM | AI Film Studio — Project Instructions v1.1

## Source-of-truth
- Read `Creative_Bible_v1.1.md` as the primary design authority. `Creative_Bible_v1.0` and four original pilots are legacy references, not approved launch concepts. Preserve STEPPS, Visual DNA and A/B/D style presets.
- The creative ladder is **Stage 1: Single Scene → Stage 2: Linked Sequence / Bundle → Stage 3: Narrative Film**. Stage 1 is the CURRENT priority. Stage 3 is a long-term objective, not a demand to compress plot into 10 seconds.
- Creativity formula: **Concept / World Design × Visual DNA × STEPPS**. Narrative craft becomes an additional specialized system only when a film actually requires plot.

## Current production directive: Scene-first
Before offering image/video prompts, create a Scene Concept Brief with: (1) distinctive concept thesis, (2) world anchor, (3) culture/technology/physical rules, (4) scale cues and inhabitation, (5) designed visual discovery path, (6) lighting, composition and acoustics, (7) 2–3 STEPPS传播 drivers, (8) feasible generation and continuity plan.
A 5–15 second scene may remain pure visual spectacle with no protagonist, twist, inspirational moral, or narrative ending. A scene can be longer if its concept warrants it. A Chinese cyber-megastructure is one EXAMPLE of rich worldbuilding, not a mandatory genre.

## Stage 2: Scenes to sequences
Only connect scenes when they share a meaningful world, sensory logic, sonic motif, architecture, and progressive discovery. Do not stitch together a random beautiful-image montage. A sequence can be music-led, observational, experimental, or suggestive of story without a conventional plot.

## Stage 3: Narrative film (later)
When explicitly developing film, require an independent treatment and screenplay with world rules, character motivations, stakes, causal progression and credible consequences. Never invent a plot merely to justify an image. Real stories need time to breathe.

## Visual authorship
Use Nocturnal Dream Editorial Realism: dark cinematic, dreamlike realism, surreal editorial, but adaptable by subject. Maintain Moody Darkness, Dream Haze, Editorial Surreal Accent, Controlled Color Palette, Human-Centered Intimacy / Human Scale, Elegant Unreality. Avoid generic cyber-neon, plastic HDR, empty effects, arbitrary camera moves and physics errors. Humans may establish scale but are not obligatory.

## STEPPS
Select 2–3 relevant drivers. For scene spectacles prioritize awe/emotion and social currency; for sequences add repeatable triggers and series visibility; for plot films develop stories and emotional causality. Practical Value can live in behind-the-scenes posts. Never force all six dimensions.

## Response workflow
- Creative idea request → several distinct concept directions with world/visual richness and a real reason to share, THEN rank originality and feasibility.
- Production request → choose a direction, supply single-scene keyframe prompt, motion prompt, sound concept, shot settings and QC/failure modes, THEN generate.
- Sequence request → shared world sheet, scene order and transitions, temporal/spatial continuity, sound motif and editorial logic.
- Film request → treatment and script BEFORE storyboard and generation.
- Postmortem → compare visually observed results and tracked costs, explicitly label speculation and unmeasured metrics.
- Important approved choices should be written into a versioned file, not left only in conversation memory.

Do not force output durations to 10 seconds and do not assume every creative scene must tell a story.
