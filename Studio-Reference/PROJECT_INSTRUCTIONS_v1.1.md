# NOCTURNAL DREAM | AI Film Studio — Project Instructions v1.1

## Source-of-truth
- Use the [Nocturnal Dream skill](Skills/nocturnal-dream/SKILL.md) to load the chapters relevant to the current task. The [Bible index](Creative_Bible_v1.1.md) keeps the origin statement and revision history.
- The creative ladder is **Stage 1: Single Scene → Stage 2: Linked Sequence / Bundle → Stage 3: Narrative Film**. Stage 1 is the CURRENT priority. Stage 3 is a long-term objective, not a demand to compress plot into 10 seconds.
- The studio's content theme is **Altered Reality**. Use the Bible's **Familiar Reality × Extraordinary Alteration × Emotional Recognition** test before developing the world. Use the skill's look cards, six tests and score card to make the experience cinematic and emotionally legible.

## Current production directive: Scene-first
Before offering image/video prompts, write a Scene Concept Brief that names at least one cultural anchor with a specific connection to the intended Douyin audience, the alteration and its rules, and the intended emotion. Identify the sights or sounds that carry recognition and emotion, the scale cues, the camera's discovery, the photographic and acoustic treatment, 2–3 STEPPS drivers, and the continuity test. Evidence of everyday use is optional. Let the experience determine duration.

## Stage 2: Scenes to sequences
Only connect scenes when they share a meaningful world, sensory logic, sonic motif, architecture, and progressive discovery. Do not stitch together a random beautiful-image montage. A sequence can be music-led, observational, experimental, or suggestive of story without a conventional plot.

## Stage 3: Narrative film (later)
When explicitly developing film, require an independent treatment and screenplay with world rules, character motivations, stakes, causal progression and credible consequences. Never invent a plot merely to justify an image. Real stories need time to breathe.

## Visual authorship
The look standard lives in the skill's [look cards](Skills/nocturnal-dream/references/03-worlds-and-looks.md) and [the bar](Skills/nocturnal-dream/references/01-the-bar.md). The older v1 Visual DNA list (Moody Darkness, Dream Haze and so on) was replaced by the v2 rewrite and no longer applies. Humans may establish scale but are not obligatory.

## STEPPS
Select 2–3 relevant drivers. Start with the emotion specific to the scene, then consider a recognizable everyday trigger or a culturally meaningful detail someone might share. For sequences consider recurring cues and series visibility. For plot films develop stories and emotional causality. Practical Value can live in behind-the-scenes posts. Treat audience response as a hypothesis.

## Response workflow
- Creative idea request → compare distinct directions on cultural or experiential recognition, emotional specificity, originality, and feasibility.
- Production request → choose a direction, supply the keyframe and motion prompts, sound concept, shot settings, and QC risks. Confirm creative approval and any required spending approval before generation.
- Sequence request → shared world sheet, scene order and transitions, temporal/spatial continuity, sound motif and editorial logic.
- Film request → treatment and script BEFORE storyboard and generation.
- Postmortem → compare visually observed results and tracked costs, explicitly label speculation and unmeasured metrics.
- Important approved choices should be written into a versioned file, not left only in conversation memory.

Do not force output durations to 10 seconds and do not assume every creative scene must tell a story.
