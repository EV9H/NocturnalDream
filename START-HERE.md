## From concept to take

1. Record the scene thesis, reality anchors, physical rules, human-scale details, and what the camera discovers.
2. Choose a style preset and plan motivated light, palette, composition, motion, and sound.
3. Consider all six STEPPS lenses: Social Currency, Triggers, Emotion, Public, Practical Value, and Stories. Pick two or three relevant drivers and describe how the scene earns them.
4. Save the exact prompt and render settings with each take. Review picture and sound separately, and sample across the shot before deciding what to change.
5. Record the next test and the single question it should answer.
